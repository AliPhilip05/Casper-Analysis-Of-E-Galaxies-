METHODS & CONTEXT

Three measures. One evolving story.

CONSELICE (2003) FRAMEWORK
CCONCENTRATION01
5 log10 r80r20

How centrally concentrated a galaxy’s light is. Higher values indicate a stronger central light profile.

AASYMMETRY02
Σ |I − I180| / Σ |I|

How much the light distribution differs after a 180° rotation. Larger values may signal disturbance.

SSMOOTHNESS03
Σ |I − IS| / Σ |I|

Small-scale structure, measured by comparing the flux with a smoothed version of itself.

!
Preliminary catalog-level analysis

The current pipeline approximates galaxy radii from shape moments, uses a one-dimensional flux reversal for asymmetry, and does not explicitly subtract background. These measurements are a foundation for future image-based validation, not a final morphological classification.
